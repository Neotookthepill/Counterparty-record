The Ledger

The Ledger records what was said, when it was said and the direction of the call. It does not invent exits or retrospective P&L. Every entry links back to the exact moment on the tape.

on the record, the calls kept to the second
We log the call, not the exit, so The Record does not score a P&L. That is on purpose. The money lives on paste.trade, where the real round trips are tracked. What The Record does that nothing else does: it writes the calls down, timestamps it to the second, and makes the whole archive indexed. The accountability is that it is on the record and cannot be memory-holed. The chart in the Academy shows what the asset did next, as a lesson, not a verdict.
